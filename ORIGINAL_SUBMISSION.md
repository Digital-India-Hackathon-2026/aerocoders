## Idea
The Problem Statement:
Every day, first-time flyers, elderly travellers, and students moving abroad struggle to interpret complex and frequently changing airline baggage rules. This leads to restricted items being packed in the wrong luggage like power banks, oversized liquids and small tools, causing financial loss at security checkpoints and slowing down checkpoint throughput for authorities like CISF. Current resources (airline PDFs, FAQ pages) are too dense for quick, practical use.

The Solution:
We propose SmartBaggage AI, a web-based prototype that helps travelers sort items correctly before they leave for the airport. The core MVP includes: 
(1) an airline-selection step that loads that carrier's baggage rules, 
(2) a camera-based image classifier that identifies a scanned item and places it into "Cabin" or "Check-in" using a pretrained computer-vision model and 
(3) a simple checklist view so users can review flagged items.
A barcode-based liquid-volume check is planned as a stretch feature if time permits.
SmartBaggage AI aims to demonstrate how a focused AI tool can meaningfully reduce traveller stress and ease airport security bottlenecks, aligning with the Digital India push for smarter public infrastructure.
